Existing stock = billets already in the yard at plan start (the order book's Billet Stock column, allocated open-stock-first). WIP on hand = material a stage has already produced that the next stage has not yet drawn.
What each stage consumes — inventory pool × consuming stage MT · consumed so far = drawn by 6 Jul · on hand = produced, awaiting the draw · planned = not yet produced
Pools: Billet stock (existing) and Cast billets feed the mill; the mill's Rolled bar is drawn by NDT, bright bar, the furnaces or dispatch depending on the order's route; each of those produces the next pool. Casting and billet draws are in billet MT (bar qty ÷ 90% mill yield); everything after the mill is in bar MT.
