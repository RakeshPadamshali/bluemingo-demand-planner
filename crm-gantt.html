Cold Rolling Mill — coil flow across units
Each coil flows Pickling → Cold Rolling → Electro-Cleaning → Annealing (Continuous CAL or Batch BAF) → Skin-Pass → optional coating (Galvanizing → Colour Coating for GI/PPGI) → finishing (Slitting or Cut-to-Length). Bars are coloured by operation; each unit processes one coil at a time.
